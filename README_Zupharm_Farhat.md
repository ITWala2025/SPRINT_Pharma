## 1. My Understanding of the Project

Zupharm Laboratories Pvt. Ltd. is a pharmaceutical company incorporated on 1st April 2025, registered in Gurugram, Haryana. The company works with WHO-GMP certified manufacturing partners and operates from two offices — Gurugram (corporate) and Okhla, New Delhi (head office).

This is not a simple information website. The website needs to serve three purposes at once:
- Establish **brand credibility** for investors and regulatory bodies
- Act as a **B2B product catalogue** for doctors and pharmacists
- Generate **PCD franchise leads** by converting visitors into enquiry form submissions

The website must communicate Zupharm's brand, products, and franchise opportunity within 5 seconds of a visitor landing on it.

## 3. Design & Branding

### Colour Palette
| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Primary Navy | #0B2545 | Headers, footer, main backgrounds |
| Primary Teal | #0D7377 | Buttons, links, section accents |
| Light Teal | #14BDAC | Hover states, highlights |
| Sky Blue | #E8F4FD | Section backgrounds (not light gray!) |
| Gold / Amber | #D4A017 | CTA buttons, badges |
| Dark Gray | #2D3748 | Body text |
| Mid Gray | #718096 | Captions, footer text |
| White | #FFFFFF | Card backgrounds |

### Typography
- **Headings** — Poppins Bold / Inter Bold
- **Body** — Inter Regular / Open Sans
- H1 — 40–48px | H2 — 28–32px | H3 — 20–24px
- Body text — 16px / 1.7 line-height

### Key UI Rules
- Max content width: 1200px centred
- Sticky navbar — logo left, links centre, "Apply for Franchise" CTA right
- Mobile-first design (320px to 1920px)
- Sticky WhatsApp button — bottom right corner, green
- AOS (Animate On Scroll) for scroll animations
- Phosphor Icons or FontAwesome for icon sections
- Breadcrumb navigation on all inner pages

## 5. Theme & Design References

### Cipla (cipla.com) — What I Liked
- **Animated stats strip** at the top showing key numbers — exactly what Zupharm needs for its counter section (500+ Products, 20+ States, etc.)
- **Human photography** in hero section — warm and approachable feel, not too corporate
- **Clean white background** with minimal design — easy to read and professional
- Simple horizontal navbar with all links clearly visible

### Sun Pharma (sunpharma.com) — What I Liked
- **Mega menu dropdown** — very organized, good reference for Zupharm's 13-page navigation
- **Gold/Orange color** used for headings and highlights — similar to Zupharm's gold CTA requirement
- **Structured contact page** — separate sections for different office addresses and department contacts — good reference for Zupharm's dual-office contact page

### Mankind Pharma (mankindpharma.com) — What I Liked
- **Rotating circular badge** — ISO-45001 certification shown as a circular spinning text badge — I want to use a similar rotating badge for Zupharm's WHO-GMP / ISO 9001:2015 certifications in the certifications section
- **Bold large typography** for section headings — impactful and confident
- **Navy blue highlight cards** — matches Zupharm's navy color scheme

### My Theme Suggestion for Zupharm
Combine:
- Cipla's **clean white layout** and **animated stats strip**
- Sun Pharma's **mega menu** and **gold accent colors**
- Mankind Pharma's **rotating certification badge** and **bold typography**
- Apply Zupharm's own **Navy + Teal + Gold** brand palette throughout
