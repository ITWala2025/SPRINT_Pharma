# 1. Website Objective & Target Audience

## Primary Goal
The Zupharm Laboratories website must serve as the company's primary digital asset — a credible, professional, and conversion-oriented platform that communicates the brand's vision, product portfolio, business model, and investment opportunity.

The site should establish trust with all stakeholder groups from day one of operations.

| Element | Font | Recommended Size |
