1. My Understanding of the Project
Zupharm Laboratories Pvt. Ltd. is a pharmaceutical company incorporated on 1st April 2025, registered in Gurugram, Haryana. The company works with WHO-GMP certified manufacturing partners and operates from two offices — Gurugram (corporate) and Okhla, New Delhi (head office).
The Zupharm Laboratories website must serve as the company's primary digital asset — a credible, professional, and conversion-oriented platform that communicates the brand's vision, product portfolio, business model, and investment opportunity. The site should establish trust with all stakeholder groups from day one of operations.
This is not a simple information website. The website needs to serve three purposes at once:

Establish brand credibility for investors and regulatory bodies
Act as a B2B product catalogue for doctors and pharmacists
Generate PCD franchise leads by converting visitors into enquiry form submissions

The website must communicate Zupharm's brand, products, and franchise opportunity within 5 seconds of a visitor landing on it. The tech stack is WordPress + Elementor Pro hosted on Hostinger Business with Cloudflare CDN.
