# Phase 1 — PCD Franchise & Products Pages Development Structure

## Phase 1 Objective
This phase focuses specifically on building the two most important business sections of the website:

- Products Section
- PCD Pharma Franchise Section

The primary goal is to establish a strong online presence, showcase the pharmaceutical product portfolio, and generate franchise leads through structured, user-friendly pages.

---

# Products Page Development Structure

## Purpose
The Products page will serve as the main catalogue for visitors, distributors, healthcare professionals, and franchise partners to explore available pharmaceutical products.

## Core Sections

### 1. Products Hero Section
- Professional banner introducing the product portfolio
- Search bar for quick product discovery
- Product catalogue download button
- Clear navigation to therapeutic categories

### 2. Product Categories Section
Products should be organized into major divisions such as:
- Cardiac & Diabetology
- Dermatology
- Orthopaedic
- Neurology
- Gynaecology
- Paediatrics
- Gastroenterology
- General Medicine

### 3. Product Listing Section
Each product card should include:
- Product image
- Brand name
- Generic composition
- Dosage form
- Category badge
- View Details button

### 4. Product Filters
Visitors should be able to filter products by:
- Therapeutic category
- Dosage type
- Availability
- Search keywords

### 5. Product Detail Page
Every individual product page should contain:
- Brand name
- Composition
- Generic name
- Dosage form
- Packing details
- Therapeutic use
- Storage instructions
- Availability
- Product enquiry form

### 6. Lead Conversion Features
- Download full catalogue option
- Product enquiry form
- Contact CTA
- CRM-ready enquiry collection

# PCD Pharma Franchise Page Development Structure

## Purpose
This page is designed as the primary lead generation page for attracting potential franchise partners.

## Core Sections

### 1. Franchise Hero Banner
- Strong business headline
- Monopoly rights highlight
- WHO-GMP certification mention
- Call-to-action button for franchise application

### 2. Why Choose Us Section
This section should clearly explain:
- Wide product range
- Monopoly-based business model
- Marketing support
- Competitive pricing
- Quality assurance
- Dedicated support

### 3. Franchise Benefits Section
Potential franchise partners should understand what they receive:
- Promotional tools
- Product visual aids
- MR support materials
- Business guidance
- Dedicated relationship manager

### 4. Investment & Eligibility Section
This should briefly explain:
- Investment expectations
- Drug licence requirements
- Business experience preference
- Available territories

### 5. Application Process Section
Simple 3-step model:
- Submit enquiry
- Business discussion
- Franchise onboarding

### 6. Franchise Enquiry Form
Required fields:
- Full name
- Mobile number
- Email
- Location
- Product interest
- Business experience
- Message

### 7. Conversion Optimization
- Fast enquiry submission
- CRM integration
- WhatsApp support
- Automated response system
